# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Nimbus to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code, kind about people. Review the change, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Ask a question before calling something wrong.
- Accept that maintainers say no. Scope decisions are not personal.
- Respect privacy: do not publish someone's logs, paths, keys, or transcripts.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or sexualized attention.
- Discriminatory jokes or comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions, or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer through https://github.com/nataaaf-code (or a private GitHub Security Advisory if you prefer). Reports are handled confidentially. Responses range from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban, depending on severity and pattern.
